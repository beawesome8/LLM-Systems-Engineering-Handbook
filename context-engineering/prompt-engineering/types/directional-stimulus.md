# Directional Stimulus Prompting

**Definition:** Steer the model's attention toward specific features, aspects, or ideas
before it performs the main task, priming the response to stay goal-aligned and focused
rather than drifting to generic coverage.

**When to use it:** Summarization or generation tasks where you know in advance which
aspects matter most (e.g. "focus on cost and timeline" before summarizing a long report) and
want to bias the model away from an unfocused, generic response.

**When not to:** Open-ended exploratory tasks where you genuinely want broad coverage — the
stimulus narrows the response, which is the opposite of what you want there.
