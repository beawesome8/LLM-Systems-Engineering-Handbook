# Deep Breath (Softened Chain of Thought)

**Definition:** A casual, non-technical phrasing of the step-by-step instruction — e.g. "Let's
take a deep breath and think through this carefully" — instead of a formal "think
step-by-step" directive. A psychological nudge rather than a technical one.

**When to use it:** Anecdotally used to soften reasoning prompts in conversational or
customer-facing contexts where a formal instruction phrase would feel out of tone. Functions
similarly to zero-shot CoT.

**When not to:** No evidence this outperforms a direct step-by-step instruction on accuracy —
treat it as a tone choice, not a reliability upgrade, until tested.

zero-shot CoT phrasing, since the claimed benefit of this technique is informal and unverified.
